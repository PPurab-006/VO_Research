**Figure 10. No visual-field variable shows a leading relationship with pose failure in these data.**
Pearson r between each variable at frame t and (a) RAW pose inliers or (b) a failure indicator (fewer than 8 inliers) at frame t + k, F9 runs 1-3 pooled (2113 frames). The largest |r| is 0.153 for the continuous target and 0.125 for the failure indicator; 39 of 144 cells have a 95% block-bootstrap interval excluding zero (unhatched). These correlations are not predictive: for the strongest cells the correlation with the target leading the variable by the same lag is of similar size (for example -0.137 against -0.148 at lag 3), i.e. they reflect the contemporaneous state of the scene. Only 7.0% of frames are failures (147 frames in 146 episodes, 145 of them a single frame), which limits what a null result can show. It does not show that no precursor exists.
