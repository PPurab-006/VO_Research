**Figure 9. Meter-valued RPE is not comparable across runs because the Sim(3) scale differs.**
(a, b) F9 run 1 only. RAW's Sim(3) scale is 0.0917 and EIS-GATED's is 0.1099. Meter RPE is 0.0838 m/step for RAW and 0.0980 for EIS-GATED; rescaling RAW by EIS-GATED's scale before a rigid alignment gives 0.0978, so the gap is a scale effect. Scale-normalised RPE (meter RPE divided by the scale, scale-invariant by construction) is 0.9143 for RAW and 0.8925 for EIS-GATED. (c) Sim(3) scale of every F9 run: values span 0.074-0.110, and EIS-GATED's is larger than RAW's in all three runs. This test concerns RPE only. Forcing EIS-GATED's scale on RAW changes RAW's ATE from 3.10 m to 3.19 m (rigid re-alignment) or 3.43 m (rescale after alignment); this does not establish whether EIS-GATED's ATE difference is real (Fig. 6).
