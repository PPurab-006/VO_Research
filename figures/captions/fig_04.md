**Figure 4. Effect of the EIS variant on F9 (yaw plus translation), three runs.**
Dots: individual runs; horizontal marks: means; dotted line: RAW mean. (a) Pose validity. FIXED (reference = first frame) falls to 62.5% and INCREMENTAL to 88.2%, against 93.0% for RAW. NULL, which sends frames through the same resampling with an identity warp, gives 93.3%, so resampling alone has no measurable cost. GATED gives 92.1%. (b) ATE (Sim(3)-aligned). Means: RAW 3.32 m, FIXED 3.43, INCREMENTAL 2.97, NULL 3.28, GATED 3.02; the between-run standard deviation within a variant (0.31-0.56 m) is comparable to or larger than the differences between variants.
