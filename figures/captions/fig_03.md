**Figure 3. Achieved yaw for the rotation families (all three runs each).**
Top: unwrapped heading from the ground-truth body x-axis. Bottom: absolute yaw rate on a 50 Hz grid (axis clipped at 320 deg/s; dotted line: the 15 deg/s gate). Heading oscillates about a mean offset of 77.8-90.7° from the start heading, with 116.8-129.8° peak to peak. 3 of 9 runs (F6 R1, F6 R3, F10 R2) contain ground-truth heading jumps above 10° between consecutive samples (largest 28.7°); they are drawn dashed and their peak rates (590.8-1119.0 deg/s) reflect these discontinuities, not vehicle motion. Median yaw rate on all runs is 22.1-42.2 deg/s.
